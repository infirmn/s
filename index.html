Frequently asked questions.

What is starcreates?

starcreates is a Roblox game development and publishing company led by co-founders and CEOs Theo Evans and Elliot Askew. We develop and operate our own Roblox experiences, and partner with, invest in and acquire games from other developers.

What Roblox games has starcreates made?

Our games include Build or Die, with over 2 million visits and a peak of 724 concurrent players, and +1 ASMR Keyboard Destruction, with over 59,800 visits. A new, larger project is currently in development. See all our games.

Who founded starcreates?

starcreates was co-founded by Theo Evans (@infirmn) and Elliot Askew (@ESGaminggg), who both serve as Co-Founder & CEO. Theo is a Roblox developer and software engineer with over 7 years of experience who has contributed to games with more than 4 billion combined play sessions. Elliot leads growth, development and community, and is a content creator with millions of views across YouTube and social media.

How many people play starcreates games?

starcreates games have recorded over 2 million play sessions, more than 342,000 players and around 24,500 monthly active users on Roblox.

Does starcreates invest in or buy Roblox games?

Yes. We are open to partnerships, Roblox game acquisitions and investing in promising projects. Email hello@starcreates.org or message @starcreates_dev on X.
